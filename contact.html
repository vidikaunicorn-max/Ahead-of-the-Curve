Based on a research paper. This tool implements the error budget of “Local mass density from Hipparcos K giants: a vertical Jeans–Poisson analysis with a mixture of isothermal components” by Vidika Mirpuri, in which ρ0 = 0.063 ± 0.016 (stat) ± 0.017 (completeness sys.) M☉ pc−3 was obtained from 3,585 stars. Forecasts are calibrated on mock catalogues generated and fitted with that paper’s pipeline.

Citing

Mirpuri, V. (2026). Local mass density from Hipparcos K giants: a vertical Jeans–Poisson analysis with a mixture of isothermal components.
